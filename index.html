How Turbo Tunez Works

1. "Need Credits?" Campaign Mode Grind

Even if nobody else is online, open Need Credits? to play and loop official label roster songs on Spotify and SoundCloud. Every 28-second verified play earns +1 Credit (with unlimited loops), which you can immediately spend on your own track promotions.

2. Community Queue + Loop Mode

Play any community track for 28 verified seconds to earn +1 Credit, or turn on ↻ Loop Track: ON to keep looping a song until its credit pool runs dry.

3. SoundCloud Bonus Engagement & Live Stats

On SoundCloud tracks where bonus actions are enabled, you can also Like, Repost, and Follow on your first pass for +1 Credit per action. Every track tracks live Plays, Likes, Reposts, and Follows.
