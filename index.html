How Turbo Tunez Works

1. 1 Credit = 1 Verified Listen

Pick any active track on the Home queue and press Play in the Spotify player. Once 28 seconds of real playback is verified, click Claim +1 Credit.

2. Set & Top Up Campaign Budgets (3 Credit Minimum)

On Promote Track, choose how many credits to put behind your track (minimum 3 credits). Whenever you earn more credits, you can add +1, +3, or a custom amount of credits straight onto any live song you submitted.

3. One Listen Per Song Per User

Every account can only earn credit from a specific track once, and you cannot earn credits from your own uploads.

4. Full-Length Playback Tip

Sign into open.spotify.com in this browser so Spotify plays full tracks instead of 30-second previews.
