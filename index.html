How Turbo Tunez Works

1. Earn Credits by Listening

Pick any active track on the Home queue and press Play in the Spotify player. Once 28 seconds of real playback is verified, click Claim +1 Credit.

2. One Listen Per Song Per User

To keep promotion real and prevent credit farming, every account can only earn credit from a specific track once, and you cannot farm credits on your own uploads.

3. Queue Your Own Releases

Head to Promote Track, paste your Spotify track URL, and spend 2 credits to receive 3 unique listens from other artists and listeners.

4. Full-Length Playback Tip

Sign into open.spotify.com in this browser so Spotify plays full tracks instead of 30-second previews.
